About

I work on broadband and polynomial-matrix beamforming — the mathematics of combining many small signals into one that points somewhere on purpose.

Most of my research lives in Laurent series, z-domain operator theory, and iterative solvers — pHCG, the algorithm at the center of my current paper, is a conjugate-gradient method built for para-Hermitian systems. This site is where that work gets a body: something you can steer, break, and rebuild in a browser instead of a MATLAB script.
